# Feels Finder

An iPhone app that asks you one thing at a time: which of these feelings is
closer to right now? Over weeks of small forced choices it builds a picture of
your inner weather: a map of where your feelings sit, a ranking of what keeps
winning, and a day-by-day trend.

## Layout

- `ios/`: the app and its widget. `project.yml` is the XcodeGen spec.
- `docs/`: the website, the support page and the privacy policy, served at
  <https://feelsfinder.app> by GitHub Pages.
- `app-store/`: App Store Connect listing copy and screenshots.
- `index.html`: early design prototype.
