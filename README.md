# Feels Finder

An iPhone app that asks you one thing at a time: which of these feelings is
closer to right now? Over weeks of small forced choices it builds a picture of
your inner weather — a map of where your feelings sit, a ranking of what keeps
winning, and a day-by-day trend.

This repository hosts the website: the support page and the privacy policy,
served at <https://brianhubbell.com/feelsfinder/> from `docs/`.

Support and bug reports: [support@feelsfinder.app](mailto:support@feelsfinder.app).
