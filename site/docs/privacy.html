Feels Finder

Privacy policy

The short version: Feels Finder collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. What you record stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud storage.

Effective 25 September 2026

What the app stores

Feels Finder records the choices you make: which feeling you picked, which feelings you picked it over, and when. That log is everything the app knows. It is kept in the app's own storage on your device.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, health data, or any device identifier, and it has no way to obtain them.

Where it goes

Your device

The log is written to the app's private container on your iPhone. Other apps cannot read it. If you delete the app, this copy is deleted with it.

Your iCloud

If your device is signed in to iCloud, the log is also backed up to your personal iCloud key-value storage, so it survives a lost phone and merges across your own devices. This storage belongs to your Apple Account. It is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and the developer of Feels Finder has no access to it whatsoever. Sign out of iCloud, or turn iCloud off for Feels Finder in Settings, and this stops.

Nowhere else

There is no Feels Finder server. The app makes no network requests of its own, contains no advertising or analytics software, and includes no third-party SDKs. No data is sold, shared, rented, or disclosed to anyone, because none of it ever reaches the developer in the first place.

Only when you say so

The app can export your log as a CSV file through the standard iOS share sheet. That happens only when you tap Export, and the file goes wherever you send it. From that point the destination you chose — Mail, Files, another app — governs what happens to it.

Tracking

Feels Finder does not track you across apps or websites, and does not use the Advertising Identifier. It will never present the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to ask permission for.

Deleting your data

  • Inside the app: Patterns → Clear my data erases the log on the device and marks it deleted in iCloud, so it is not restored on your other devices.
  • One entry at a time: swiping back to a previous card takes back the answer you gave it.
  • Everything: deleting the app removes the on-device copy. To remove the iCloud copy as well, clear your data in the app first, or delete the app's data under Settings → your name → iCloud.

Children

Feels Finder is not directed at children under 13, and collects no personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, any change would be a narrowing rather than a widening of what is described above.
